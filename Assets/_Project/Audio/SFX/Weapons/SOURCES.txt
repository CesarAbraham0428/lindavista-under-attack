Prepared clips used by the game (Prepared/):
- Pistol_Shot.wav: first shot only, original 0.035-1.015 s (0.980 s including its decay).
- AssaultRifle_Shot.wav: first shot only, original 0.036-0.335 s (0.299 s including its decay).
- RPG_Shot.wav: one launch, original 0.010-2.210 s (2.200 s including its decay).
- Pistol_Reload.wav, AssaultRifle_Reload.wav, RPG_Reload.wav: each original reload converted to 1.200 s using pitch-preserving tempo adjustment.
All prepared clips are mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV, normalized to -3 dBFS with short fades to avoid edit clicks.
WeaponAudioLibrary references only these six prepared clips; the original recordings are not played in gameplay.
